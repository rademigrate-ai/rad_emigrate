# 13 — RISK AND MITIGATION REGISTER

| Risk ID | Identified Risk Scenario | Severity | Likelihood | Architectural Mitigation | Verification Evidence |
|:---:|:---|:---:|:---:|:---|:---|
| **RSK-01** | AI Research Assistant auto-publishes inaccurate immigration criteria directly to users. | **CRITICAL** | Low | Structural separation: accepting research proposals creates unapproved drafts (`editorial_state = 'draft'`). AI workers have zero write access to published tables. | `no_ai_research_auto_publish_test.dart`, `admin_cms_test.dart` |
| **RSK-02** | Schema drift between backend PostgreSQL RPC functions and Flutter client data models. | **HIGH** | Medium | Dual-sided JSON Schema registry (`rad_v1.json`). All client requests and responses pass through `requireRadContract()`. Violations fail closed. | `pr74_remediation_test.dart`, `admin_plan4_research_cms_test.dart` |
| **RSK-03** | Editorial concurrency collisions: simultaneous edits overwriting colleagues' work. | **HIGH** | Medium | Optimistic locking via `expected_revision`. Conflicts raise PostgreSQL 409, translated to `CmsException('conflict')`. Unsaved text is preserved in form controllers. | `admin_cms_test.dart` (selected editor keeps unsaved text) |
| **RSK-04** | SSRF or unauthorized crawl targeting internal infrastructure or metadata APIs. | **CRITICAL** | Low | Egress domain restriction against `Authority.official_domains`. Private IP ranges (`10.0.0.0/8`, `127.0.0.1`, `169.254.169.254`) blocked at DNS & network layers. | `research_source_url_test.dart` |
| **RSK-05** | Pre-publication drafts or unverified proposals leaked to public search queries or newsfeed. | **HIGH** | Low | Public query views (`get_visa_article`, `search_visa_articles`) filter strictly on `editorial_state = 'published' AND is_active = true`. | `article_change_highlights_test.dart`, `canonical_pathway_test.dart` |
| **RSK-06** | Urgent legal injunction requires instantaneous retraction of published criteria. | **HIGH** | Low | Atomic `rollback_visa_article` RPC restores historical verified revision as a new head revision within sub-second RTO. | `admin_plan4_research_cms_test.dart` |
| **RSK-07** | Infinite cursor loops or denial-of-service via malformed pagination envelopes. | **MEDIUM** | Low | Client-side cursor cycle detector tracks visited cursor tokens with a hard ceiling of `maxPagesPerList = 200`. Cycles throw `CmsException('malformed')`. | `admin_cms_test.dart` (multi-page cursor cycle rejection) |
