# Visa Editorial Policy

1. Never invent eligibility, fees, processing times, scores, quotas, or guarantees.
2. Prefer empty requirements/steps + honest UI pending state over filler steps.
3. RAD content establishes service/catalog framing, not legal authority for current rules.
4. Official regulatory claims require government authority evidence (class B).
5. Neutral catalog copy (class C) must not be labeled as regulatory verification.
6. Conflicts between RAD pages and official rules must not be silently overwritten.
7. Prefer durable language ("confirm current official rules") over brittle numbers.
8. Persian and English should both be professional; do not machine-translate unverified regulatory claims.
